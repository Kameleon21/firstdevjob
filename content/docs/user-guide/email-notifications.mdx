# Email Notifications

Never miss a new job posting. Opt in to email notifications and we'll alert you whenever new approved jobs match your role preferences.

## How to Enable

1. Sign in and navigate to your **Profile** page
2. Select the **Notifications** tab
3. Toggle **Email Notifications** on

That's it — you'll start receiving emails for newly approved jobs.

## Role Filters

After enabling notifications, you can filter by role level:

- **Intern** — internship and placement roles
- **Graduate** — graduate-level positions
- **Early Career** — junior roles for developers with some experience

Select one or more filters to only receive notifications for those role types. If you leave all filters unchecked, you'll be notified about all new jobs.

## How to Unsubscribe

You can stop receiving notifications in two ways:

1. **From your profile** — go to Profile > Notifications and toggle off
2. **From any email** — click the **unsubscribe** link at the bottom of any notification email

## Tips

- Combine role filters to match exactly what you're looking for
- Check your spam folder if you've enabled notifications but aren't receiving emails
- You can toggle notifications on and off at any time without losing your filter preferences
