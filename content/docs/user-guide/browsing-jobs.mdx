# Browsing Jobs

FirstDevJob makes it easy to find relevant job listings for your skill level.

## Search & Filters

Use the search bar and filters to narrow down jobs:

- **Search** - Find jobs by title, company, or keywords
- **Location** - Filter by remote, on-site, or specific locations
- **Job Type** - Full-time, part-time, internship, contract
- **Tags** - Filter by technologies (React, Python, etc.)

## Job Cards

Each job card displays:

- Company name and logo
- Job title
- Location and job type
- Posted date
- Key tags/technologies

## Bookmarking Jobs

Click the bookmark icon to save jobs for later. Access your bookmarks from the navigation menu.
