# Browsing Jobs

FirstDevJob makes it easy to find relevant job listings for your skill level.

## Search & Filters

Use the search bar and filters to narrow down jobs:

- **Search** - Find jobs by title, company, or keywords
- **Position Level** - Filter by intern, graduate, or early career roles
- **Location** - Filter by posting location values

## Job Cards

Each job card displays:

- Company name and logo
- Job title
- Location and position level
- Posted date
- Key tags/technologies

## Bookmarking Jobs

Click the bookmark icon to save jobs for later. Access your bookmarks from the navigation menu.
Tracked jobs remain visible in your dashboard even after a posting closes or is removed, so you can continue tracking your hiring progress.
